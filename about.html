From a platform to a national portfolio.

A Saudi-led team that turned field expertise into eight world-class cybersecurity platforms, trusted by government entities and sensitive sectors across the Kingdom.

  1. 2022
    2022

    A National Beginning

    Advanced Controls IT Co. was established in Riyadh in 2022 by a Saudi team that saw the expertise it had gained protecting organizations deserved to become a product. The first thing we built was CyberMode, a Saudi-built platform unifying the full scope of cybersecurity management, from governance, risk and compliance through to incidents, vulnerabilities and awareness, in a single system.

    CyberMode · Founded

  2. 2023
    2023

    Our First Clients and a Wider Portfolio

    CyberMode entered the market and reached our first clients, a platform developed entirely within the Kingdom. In parallel, we launched the rest of our offering across consulting, compliance and specialized services, so that Saudi organizations could find the platform and the national expertise that runs it in one place, with local support and a team in the same time zone.

    First Clients · Consulting Launch

  3. 2024
    2024

    A Year of Growth and Presence

    Our client base expanded, and we earned the confidence of government entities and sensitive sectors project after project, delivered by Saudi professionals with field experience protecting critical organizations. That growth earned us our place at Black Hat MEA in Riyadh, a Saudi company presenting its own technology on its own ground.

    Black Hat MEA · Gov't Trust

  4. 2025
    2025

    From a Platform to a Portfolio

    What began as a single platform became a full portfolio: governance, data, IT service management, threat intelligence, security assessment, secure file transfer, and air-gapped transfer. Each solution came from a real operational need we observed in the field, engineered by a Saudi-led team with global-caliber expertise. We launched them all from the Black Hat MEA stage, in the same year we obtained our international accreditations: ISO 27001, ISO 22301, ISO 31000, ISO 9001:2015 and CREST.

    ISO 27001 · CREST · Full Portfolio

  5. 2026
    2026

    Eight Saudi-Built Platforms. One National Mission.

    We renewed our international accreditations, and our portfolio now stands at eight Saudi-built platforms alongside a complete consulting and services practice. We are building more than software, we are creating scalable Saudi technology assets designed to empower organizations, advance digital transformation, strengthen cybersecurity, and generate sustainable long-term value, in full alignment with Saudi Vision 2030. We are preparing to return to Black Hat MEA for a third consecutive year.

    8 Platforms · Vision 2030
