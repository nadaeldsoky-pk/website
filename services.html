A Consistent Four-Phase Delivery.

Every engagement follows a structured approach from analysis through to operations and support, so each deliverable is evidence-based and each improvement is measurable.

01

Analysis and Assessment

Understand the business environment, identify information assets, analyze the current state, and assess cybersecurity maturity against national and international frameworks.

02

Evaluation and Planning

Assess likelihood and evaluate financial, operational, legal, reputational and security impact. Define priorities and classify findings by severity.

03

Framework and Documentation

Develop the policies, registers and treatment plans with assigned responsibilities and controls, and build the roadmap, reports and dashboards with KPIs and KRIs.

04

Operations and Support

Perform periodic updates, review control effectiveness, verify compliance, and report regularly to senior leadership.
